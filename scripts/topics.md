  Time & calendar (Days of the week, months of the year, seasons, times of day (dawn, dusk, midnight, noon))
  - Numbers — cardinal (one through twenty, hundred, thousand) and ordinal (first, second...)                                                       
                                                            
  The body
  - Body parts (head, hand, eye, ear, heart, blood, bone...)
  - Emotions (joy, grief, anger, fear, pride, shame, envy, longing)

  The natural world
  - Weather (rain, snow, wind, storm, thunder, lightning, fog)
  - Terrain (mountain, river, forest, swamp, desert, shore, cliff)
  - Animals — especially ones relevant to Drow culture (spider, bat, snake, rat, wolf, crow, lizard)

  Underdark-specific
  - Underground features (cavern, tunnel, stalactite, mushroom, fungus, phosphorescence)
  - Darkness and light (shadow, flame, ember, candle, torch, glow)

  Material culture
  - Metals and materials (iron, steel, silver, gold, stone, crystal, silk, leather, bone)
  - Food and drink (bread, meat, broth, fruit, water, wine, venom, mushroom)
  - Clothing and adornment (cloak, robe, boots, ring, chain, veil)

  Social & political
  - Ranks and roles (slave, servant, guard, spy, assassin, merchant, noble, herald, elder)
  - Kinship terms (mother, father, son, daughter, brother, sister, ancestor, heir)

  Combat & magic
  - Weapons and tactics (dagger, bow, poison, trap, ambush, retreat, surrender)
  - Magic concepts (spell, ritual, curse, illusion, silence, void, soul, shadow)

  The most bang-for-buck ones are probably numbers, body parts, emotions, and kinship — those are near-universal in any conversation and would
  frequently hit the algorithmic fallback right now. Underdark terrain and darkness vocabulary would be the most setting-flavored additions.
